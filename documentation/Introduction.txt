- Affected zones
- Population
- High risk/ medical patients
- Shelters
- Hospitals
- Ambulances
- Food Water Resources
- Road Conditions
- Blocked Roads
- Distances
- Resource Availability

It will produce decisions scuh as:

Priority 1 :
Evacuate high risk medical patients
Priority 2:
Route Civilians to shelter 8
Priority 3:
Deploy ambulances 3,7 and 11
Priority 4:
Allocate water resources to zone 4

It will also provide an explanation/ rule trace

Medical Urgency --> High
Population Risk --> High
Shelter Capacity --> Available
Road Accessibility --> Available
Distance --> Low

Therefore:
Zone 4 recieves highest evacuation Priority
